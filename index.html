Privacy manifesto

Your files are none of our business.

Spectra was built on a simple rule: a cleaning app should never need the internet. Scanning, hashing and cleaning all happen on your Mac, and stay there.

Local processing

Every byte is analyzed on-device. Nothing is uploaded, indexed remotely, or "improved with cloud AI".

No account. No telemetry.

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing phoning home. We can't see your data — by design, not by policy.

Open report format

Exports are plain, self-contained HTML you can open in any browser and audit line by line. No lock-in.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is Spectra really 100% local?
Yes. Scanning, hashing, cleaning and reporting all run on your Mac and the results stay there. Spectra needs no account, sends no telemetry, and works perfectly with the network cable unplugged.
Can Spectra delete something important?
Spectra never bypasses the Trash. Every delete is itemized, explicitly confirmed by you, and lands in the Trash where it can be restored. Ignore lists let you fence off anything that must never be touched.
Is Pro really a one-time purchase?
Really. Spectra Pro is US$11.99 once — a launch price, regularly $16.99 — and it's yours for life. No subscription ever, and a 15-day money-back guarantee if it's not for you.
What does my Mac need to run it?
macOS 12 Monterey or later. Spectra v1.0.0 ships as a universal build, native on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
