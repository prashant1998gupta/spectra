Purchase FAQ

The small print, in big type.

What if Spectra Pro isn't for me?
Every purchase comes with a 15-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Reply to your license email within 15 days of buying and we refund you in full — you keep using the free tier as long as you like.
How many Macs does one license cover?
One Spectra Pro license activates up to 3 Macs — say, your desktop, your laptop and the family iMac. Replacing a machine? Deactivate the old one from within the app and activate the new one; the count never gets stuck.
How do I receive my license?
By email, within minutes of purchase. You'll get a license key and a receipt; paste the key into Spectra once and you're done. No account is ever created — the key itself is your proof of purchase, so keep the email safe. Lost it? Open a support request and we'll resend it — mention that it's a lost key, but never post your purchase address publicly; we'll ask for it privately.
Is there a student or education discount?
Yes — students, teachers and academic staff get 30% off Spectra Pro. Write to support from your institutional email address (or attach proof of enrollment) and we'll send you a personal discount code.
Will I ever be charged again?
No. Spectra Pro is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription and never will be. Your license includes all updates in the 1.x series. If a paid major upgrade ever ships years from now, it will be optional, discounted for existing owners, and your current version keeps working forever.
